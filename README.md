# Super-Store-Data-Analysis
# Superstore Sales & Financial Performance Analysis

An end-to-end data analytics project exploring retail sales and profitability using Excel, SQL, and Power BI[cite: 1].

---

## 📌 What is This Project?
This project walks through a complete data analysis workflow using a retail Superstore dataset[cite: 1]. The goal is simple: take messy transactional sales data, clean it up, ask smart business questions using SQL, and turn the numbers into an easy-to-understand interactive dashboard[cite: 1].

---

## 🛠️ How It Was Built
* **Microsoft Excel**: Used to review the raw data, clean columns, fix date formats, and run quick calculations using Pivot Tables[cite: 1].
* **PostgreSQL / SQL**: Used to run deeper queries—grouping sales by region, finding top-performing products, and calculating average order values[cite: 1].
* **Microsoft Power BI**: Used to build a one-page dashboard with dynamic filters and charts so anyone can explore the numbers visually[cite: 1].

---

## 🔍 Key Findings
* **The Big Numbers**: The store generated over **$2.3M in total sales** and roughly **$286K–$292K in total profit** across 5,000+ orders[cite: 1].
* **Top Category**: **Technology** brought in the highest sales, outperforming Furniture and Office Supplies[cite: 1].
* **Best Region**: The **West** led all regions with ~$725K in sales, followed by the East, Central, and South regions[cite: 1].
* **Main Customer Type**: Individual **Consumers** bought far more than Corporate or Home Office clients[cite: 1].
* **Monthly Patterns**: Sales fluctuated noticeably throughout the year, showing clear seasonal peaks[cite: 1].

---

## 🖥️ What’s on the Dashboard?
* **Quick KPI Cards**: Glanceable totals for Sales, Profit, Quantity, Total Orders, and Average Order Value[cite: 1].
* **Sales vs. Profit by Category**: Shows which categories actually make money versus those that just have high volume[cite: 1].
* **Regional Map**: A geographic look at where orders are coming from[cite: 1].
* **Top 10 Products**: A quick ranking of the items driving the most revenue[cite: 1].
* **Monthly Trends**: A timeline chart showing when sales spike or drop[cite: 1].
* **Interactive Slicers**: Clickable buttons to filter everything instantly by Year, Region, Category, or Customer Segment[cite: 1].

---

## 💡 Practical Takeaways
1. **Watch the margins, not just revenue**: A product can bring in lots of cash but very little actual profit[cite: 1].
2. **Never run out of top sellers**: Protect inventory for the Top 10 products so high-demand items don't go out of stock[cite: 1].
3. **Fix low-performing areas**: Study why the South lags behind the West to see if marketing or shipping needs improvement[cite: 1].
4. **Plan around seasons**: Use monthly sales spikes to time marketing campaigns and stock up ahead of busy months[cite: 1].
